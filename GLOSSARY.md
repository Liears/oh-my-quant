# WhyJ Quant Research

Terms used when assessing quantitative research and its results.

## Language

**Verified calculation**:
A calculation independently reproduced from matching archived observations and task parameters. Verification establishes agreement with those observations, rather than the accuracy of the provider or a forecast of future performance.

**Research task**:
A declared research goal with a scope, constraints and acceptance criteria. Satisfying its calculation criteria and delivering a complete explanation are separate requirements.

**Answer contradiction**:
An explicit statement that conflicts with the current research evidence. A correctly stated limitation or a qualification of another metric is not a contradiction.

**Principal return**:
The simulated final value less cumulative gross contributions, divided by those contributions. It is a plan-level cumulative profit ratio, not an annualized return.

**Time-weighted return (TWR)**:
The return of a performance index adjusted for external contributions. Its interpretation depends on the valuation window, fees and treatment of distributions.

**TWR maximum drawdown**:
The largest observed peak-to-trough decline of the cash-flow-adjusted performance index. Zero observed drawdown does not establish absence of risk between observations or in the future.
